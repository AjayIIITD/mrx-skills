5. RETENTION ANALYSIS (3/10)

Likely retention pattern:

  • 0:00-0:02: ~40% swipe-away (first frame doesn't stop the scroll)
  • 0:02-0:05: ~20% leave (animation quality/pacing doesn't hook)
  • 0:05-0:15: ~15% leave (story setup too slow for Shorts format)

7. CONTENT STRATEGY

The single biggest mistake: 1 Short/day getting 10-41 views. You need to COMPLETELY change your approach.

Recommended Strategy:

  1. Stop daily Shorts. Start weekly long-form (3-5 min). Long-form has better CPM, better search discovery, better retention curves. YouTube rewards watch time, not upload count.
  2. Pick Tillu as your mascot. Make Tillu a recurring character. Create "Tillu Ki Kahaniyan" playlist. This gives people a reason to subscribe.

  4. Use Shorts as TRAILERS for long-form, not main content. 15-second teaser → "पूरी कहानी के लिए लिंक पर क्लिक करें" → drives to long-form.
